Who can sign in

Everyone needs an account to open the site. Reps can use the matrix and make RFQs. Editors can also change burners. Admins can also add and remove people. Name, phone and email fill in “Prepared by” on RFQs; people can change their own from My profile on the main page.

Settings

The address the Email PDF button fills in for every RFQ. Separate several with commas. Reps can still change it on a single RFQ. Leave blank to use the container's RFQ_TO setting.

An RFQ that's Emailed or Quoted gets flagged “Follow up” after this many days with no activity (no email, status change or “Followed up” click). Use 0 to turn reminders off.
